How to Play (Beginner Mode)

Goal: Be the first to complete a hand of 4 sets + 1 pair (14 tiles total).

Sets are either three identical tiles (triplet) or three consecutive numbers of the same suit (sequence — sequences only count for Characters, Bamboo, and Dots). Winds and Dragons can only form triplets.

Your turn: A tile is drawn automatically for you. Click a tile in your hand (bottom row) to discard it.

Calling tiles: When any player discards a tile you need, you may be offered Pung (claim it to complete a triplet from a matching pair in your hand) or Win (claim it to complete your winning hand). Buttons appear when available — respond quickly or it will be skipped!

Self-draw win: If the tile you draw completes your hand, you'll be offered the Win button immediately.

This simplified beginner mode skips Chow (sequence calls), Kong, flowers, and detailed fan scoring — first valid hand simply wins the round.
